This inventory is derived from package-lock.json and installed package manifests; it is not a claim that every dependency is included in every generated website. The current static HTML renderer does not embed dependency JavaScript in published HTML. The editor distribution and future animation runtime require their own bundle-level review before release.

## Reviewed compatibility policy

MIT, ISC, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, Apache-2.0, CC0-1.0, 0BSD, and Python-2.0 are accepted with applicable copyright, notice, disclaimer, and attribution requirements. Dual expressions are parsed: OR permits a compatible branch and AND requires all obligations. JSZip is used under its MIT option.

The following explicit additions are compatible with MIT licensing of original HowlScene code, subject to their separate obligations:

- MPL-2.0: axe-core, @axe-core/playwright, lightningcss and its platform packages. Preserve MPL notices and make covered source available when distributing covered executable code. Modifications to covered files remain subject to MPL. HowlScene does not modify these dependency sources. The accessibility packages are test tooling; lightningcss is build tooling.
- BlueOak-1.0.0: minimatch. Preserve its license and notices when redistributing.
- CC-BY-4.0: caniuse-lite data. Attribute the upstream project and contributors, link the license, and indicate modifications where applicable. This is build-time browser compatibility data.
- Zlib, in conjunction with MIT: pako. Preserve notices, do not misrepresent origin, and mark altered source versions. Both license obligations apply.

This is an engineering compatibility review, not legal advice or a claim of legal exclusivity. Unknown licenses and copyleft-only licenses outside this reviewed set fail the automated gate. Review source-offer and notice placement for the actual distribution before release.
